# Metric space
$M=(X,d)$ where $d:X \times X \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ for $\forall x,y,z \in X$ adheres to the metric postulates of non-negativity, symmetry, identity and triangle inequality [(Similarity Search)](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/0-387-29151-2).
