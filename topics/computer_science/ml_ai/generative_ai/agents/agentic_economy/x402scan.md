# x402scan
- The x402 **block explorer, analytics dashboard, and marketplace** for paid APIs and agentic commerce ([x402scan](https://www.x402scan.com/))
- Indexes on-chain x402 activity so buyers, sellers, and agents can discover services and inspect real usage before integrating
## Views
- **Marketplace** — curated and self-listed x402 services with live activity, volume, and pricing
- **Transactions** — individual on-chain x402 payments
- **Facilitators** — the verify/settle intermediaries processing payments
- **Networks** — per-chain breakdown (USDC on Base and Solana dominate)
- **Ecosystem** — aggregate rollups of the whole x402 economy
