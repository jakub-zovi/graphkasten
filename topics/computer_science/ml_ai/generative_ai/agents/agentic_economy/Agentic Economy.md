# Agentic Economy
- The emerging economy in which AI agents **discover, pay for, and sell services autonomously** — machine-to-machine commerce settled on-chain, without accounts, API keys, or human approval in the loop
- The stack has three layers: **payment rails** (protocols), **discovery** (directories and explorers), and **marketplaces** (where supply and demand meet)
