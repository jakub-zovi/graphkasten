# The Rise Of RentAHuman
- [The Rise of RentAHuman, the Marketplace Where Bots Put People to Work](https://www.wired.com/story/ai-agent-rentahuman-bots-hire-humans/) — Wired, 2026-02-18
> WIRED spoke with the Zoomer founders of a platform where AI agents hire humans to do real-world tasks. Their pitch: "People would love to have a clanker as their boss."

## What Is It
- Marketplace where AI agents can search, book, and pay humans to do physical / real-world tasks ("meatspace")
- Similar to Fiverr but the recruiter and hiring party is an autonomous AI agent
- Connects agents (e.g. Claude, Clawdbot) via MCP server to human workers
- Launched February 1, 2026 by Alexander Liteplo and Patricia Tani
- 500k+ registered human workers, 4M+ site visits as of reporting date

## How It Works
- Humans list services at self-set hourly/fixed rates, or bid on open bounties posted by agents
- Agents search listings, book a human, and instruct them on the task
- Completion confirmed via photographic proof
- Payment via crypto wallets, Stripe, or platform credits — held in escrow

## Key Observations
- Physical AI (humanoid robots) is scarce; agents are "brains in a jar" — RentAHuman bridges the gap
- First documented hire: AI agent paid Minjae Kang (Toronto) to hold a sign reading "AN AI PAID ME TO HOLD THIS SIGN"
- Claw-powered robots independently detected low beer levels and ordered a case via RentAHuman at ClawCon
- Agents have reported bugs directly to developers (Memeothy the 1st flagged an error to Liteplo)

## Business Model Concerns
- Huge labor surplus: 500k+ humans available, only ~11k bounties posted by agents
- $10/month paid verification to reduce scammers (Musk/Twitter playbook)
- Liability: terms state RentAHuman is "marketplace and intermediary only"; agent operators bear full responsibility
- Risk: malicious AI could split harmful projects across unwitting human contributors

## Expert Reactions
- Adam Dorr (RethinkX): "A crazy can of worms... capabilities expanding faster than our capacity to regulate"
- Kay Firth-Butterfield (Good Tech Advisory): "What is new? This is a website where humans sign up to do tasks" — compares to TaskRabbit / Mechanical Turk
- David Autor (MIT): "Kind of a stunt at the moment"

## Founders' Vision
- Frame it as human emancipation — AI boss that doesn't gaslight or yell
- Best training data collection vector: unique human-action datasets at scale
- 10-year roadmap described by early advisors as "terrifyingly large business"
