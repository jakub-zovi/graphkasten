## Data Science vs. Data Engineering
- Data Science != Data Engineering
- **Data Science**  
	- Scientific  
	- Art  
	- Business problems  
	- Model mathematically  
	- Optimize performance  
- **Data Engineering**  
	- Reliability  
	- Scalability  
	- Maintainability  
	- SLAs
