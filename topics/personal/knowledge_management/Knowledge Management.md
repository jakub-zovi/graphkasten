# Knowledge Management
[Personal Knowledge Management](https://medium.com/@theo-james/personal-knowledge-management-a-guide-to-tools-and-systems-ebc6b56f63ca):
> Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) is all about taking control of the endless stream of information we encounter daily. Instead of drowning in emails, articles, and random notes, PKM helps you collect, organize, and make sense of it all — so you can actually use that knowledge instead of just hoarding it like a digital squirrel.
