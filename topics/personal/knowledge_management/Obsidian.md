## Hotkeys
- `Ctrl/Cmd + P` — Command Palette
- `Ctrl/Cmd + O` — Quick Switcher
- `Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + F` — Search across vault
- `Ctrl/Cmd + G` — Open Graph View
- `Ctrl/Cmd + E` — Toggle edit/preview mode
- `Ctrl/Cmd + ,` — Open Settings
