# Profitability Ratios
> Profitability is a key aspect to analyze when considering an investment in a company. This is because high [revenues](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/r/revenue.asp) alone don't necessarily translate into high earnings or high [dividends](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/d/dividend.asp).
> 
> Essentially, profitability analysis seeks to determine whether a company will make a profit. It examines business productivity from multiple angles using a few different scenarios.
