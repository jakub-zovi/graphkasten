# Shiller PE ratio (CAPE Ratio)

> The CAPE ratio is a valuation measure that uses real earnings per share (EPS) over a 10-year period to smooth out fluctuations in corporate profits that occur over different periods of a business cycle.
> 
> The CAPE ratio, an acronym for cyclically adjusted price-to-earnings ratio, was popularized by Yale University professor Robert Shiller. It is also known as the Shiller P/E ratio.

$$
\text{CAPE Ratio} = \frac{\text{Share Price}}{\text{10-year average, inflation-adjusted earnings}}
$$
