# Incerto
- Five-volume philosophical essay by [[Nassim Nicholas Taleb]] on uncertainty, luck, probability, and decision-making under opacity. Published between 2001 and 2018. Each volume is self-contained and can be read in any order.
> An investigation of opacity, luck, uncertainty, probability, human error, risk, and decision making when we don't understand the world — expressed in the form of a personal essay with autobiographical sections, stories, parables, and philosophical, historical, and scientific discussions.
## Books
- [[Fooled by Randomness]]: The Hidden Role of Chance in Life and in the Markets
	- 2001
	- How luck is systematically mistaken for skill in markets and life; the hidden role of chance in outcomes we attribute to ability
	- Recommended in 16 - Appreciating the Role of Luck
- [[The Black Swan]]: The Impact of the Highly Improbable
	- 2007
	- Highly improbable, unpredictable events with massive impact — and our post-hoc tendency to rationalize them as predictable; ranked by The Sunday Times as one of the 12 most influential books since WWII
- [[The Bed of Procrustes]]: Philosophical and Practical Aphorisms
	- 2010
	- Collection of aphorisms on the human tendency to force reality into pre-existing categories rather than adapting our models to the world
- [[Antifragile - Things That Gain from Disorder]]
	- 2012
	- Things that gain from volatility, stress, and disorder — the opposite of fragile; argues for building systems and lives that benefit from uncertainty rather than merely withstanding it
- [[Skin in the Game]]: Hidden Asymmetries in Daily Life
	- 2018
	- Symmetry and fairness through having personal stakes in outcomes; critiques those who make decisions without bearing the consequences
