# Seeking Alpha
## Price
- $299/year - not worth at all in my opinion, reports and analyses can be even quite wrong
	- I am currently on first month plan for $5, but I will definitely not go to the full subscription 
## Features
- Automatically generated reports
	- Provide very good introduction to individual businesses
	- Also contains positives and negative summaries about the stock
## Missing Features
- Does not cover European stocks
- **Unable to export financial data to CSV**
	- **This is deal breaker for me**
## Premium Features
- Unlimited access to expert investor content
- A community of serious investors
- Seeking Alpha's Quant Ratings - our 'Strong Buy' ratings outperform the S&P 500
- Rankings of Top Stocks and ETFs to help you identify great opportunities
- Stock and ETF screeners
- A portfolio health check and warnings to help you become a more informed investor
